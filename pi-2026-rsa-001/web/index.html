Bottom line

MODERATE Composite 37 / 100 · Tier 3 of 5

The neighborhood is safe. The run is the risk. Across the trailing year, nothing violent was reported anywhere on the Lake Harriet parkway. Eighteen events surfaced within five miles of the house, and the closest of any kind was a car crash about a mile away.

Almost the entire score comes from one thing: running alone, in darkness, on a fixed route at a fixed time, across a seam where two police departments each cover only part of the ground. Remove the pre-dawn run and the score falls to 27.

Scope

Two filters, applied in order

Date. The event happened between 14 September 2025 and 14 September 2026. When a story was published never qualifies it; only when the event occurred. Distance. The event happened within roughly five miles of the house. Distances are estimated from street geography.

What the one-year limit costs

The strict window removes the single closest match that exists for this question. In September 2015 a solo runner was grabbed from behind and chased at 5:30 a.m. on West Lake Harriet Parkway near Sheridan Avenue South. Same time, same place, same activity. It also removes a series of escalating incidents on this lake in July 2017, and a jogger struck by a car on this parkway in November 2020.

Those three are the most relevant events on record for this location. The resulting picture is less informative about the question being asked, not safer. The vulnerability score is now carried by structural conditions rather than by precedent.

The parkway itself

Nothing was reported on the running surface

No violent incident, robbery, assault or exposure report was located on the Lake Harriet parkway or its adjoining blocks at any point in the 365-day window. The search was run repeatedly and from several angles: the parkway by name, the band shell, the boat launch, the recreation center, Park Police incident summaries, and neighborhood press. Every Lake Harriet result returned either an incident from 2023 or earlier, or concert programming.

This is a null result and it is reported as one. It means no incident was reported in an accessible source. It is not a certification that none occurred, and it cannot become one until the outstanding records requests return. The City's incident-level crime dataset, which is the record that would actually settle the question, could not be reached from the working environment.

Sensitivity test

Hold the site and intelligence categories constant and reduce vulnerability to its ambient residue of roughly 18, which is what remains once activity exposure, route predictability, construction and the offender window fall away and the patrol seam stops applying to a resident indoors at 5:30. The composite becomes 27, which is Tier 4 Low.

Scope test

The score was computed at neighborhood scope and again at five miles, and reproduced the same band both times. Widening the radius moved the composite by one point. Across five scopes and a quality-control pass the score ran 34, 36, 36, 37, 37. Geography is not what drives this assessment. The behavior is.

Scoring is analyst judgment adapted from the Best Buy executive-protection threat matrix. It is a directional indicator, not a calibrated instrument. A three-point spread sits inside its noise, and the band is the finding rather than the number.

The transferable lesson

Date verification, not collection volume, was the binding constraint on this assessment. Search-result summaries proved unreliable on dates specifically, attaching a current frame to old reporting. Every one of the six would have passed a casual read, and each described exactly the kind of incident being asked about.

One correction is worth stating plainly: the Linden Hills figure survived into two published revisions of this document before it was caught. It is recorded here rather than quietly removed.

One exclusion is named so it is visible rather than silent. The Annunciation church shooting occurred roughly 1.5 miles away, comfortably inside the radius, and drives the 5th Precinct's year-over-year shooting-victim increase on its own. It happened on 27 August 2025, eighteen days before this window opens, so it does not qualify. Excluded on distance: the Elm Creek Park Reserve assault at roughly 15 miles, and the North Minneapolis pursuit crash at roughly 7 miles.

Sentiment

What the neighborhood itself says

The complaint is crowding, not danger

The Lake Harriet loop trail carries more than 1,600 public reviews, with usage repeatedly described as heavy. A corridor residents avoid does not generate that pattern. Coverage of the break-in wave captured the counter-current: "We think we live in a bubble here, but I guess not."

The 2026 civic agenda is not about crime

Neighborhood association and local press coverage through the year centers on the Lynnhurst Park master plan and its wading pool, the Oliver Avenue sewer works, Penn Avenue bridge redecking, and Porchfest. Residents organizing around park amenities rather than safety is what a low-threat neighborhood looks like from the inside.

The resident-forum layer was not collected

Nextdoor sits behind a login wall. Reddit refuses this kind of automated access outright, returning a hard refusal naming the user agent, so no amount of retrying reaches it. These are the one source that would surface incidents too small to reach the press, which is precisely the gap the parkway null result leaves open. They have to be searched from the owner's own browser, recording for every hit the event date and whether the account is firsthand or a repost.

Action

Four checks before treating this as settled

  • Walk the route once at 5:30 a.m., this season. No database substitutes for it. Lighting, blind spots, how isolated the darkest stretch feels, phone signal, and how the Oliver Avenue works change things. A pleasant summer walk does not answer a January question.
  • File two records requests, not one. City police cover the house. Park Board police cover the parkway actually run on. A request to the city alone misses the running surface entirely. Both drafts are written, and the Park Board request also asks how many patrol hours the lake receives between 4 and 7 a.m., which would measure the coverage seam rather than infer it.
  • Search Nextdoor and Reddit personally. Record the event date and firsthand-versus-repost status for every hit. Applying that rule at collection time is what prevents a repeat of the quality-control findings above.
  • Run the two offender-registry searches against the address. Both are quick interactive lookups.

One open question that changes how this reads

The runner's own risk profile is not on record, and it determines how heavily the pre-window precedents, particularly the 2015 assault at this exact time and place, should weigh against the clean in-window parkway record. No other open item changes the tier.
