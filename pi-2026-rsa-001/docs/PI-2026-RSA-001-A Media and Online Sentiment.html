03 Sentiment

The lake reads as safe and heavily used, and the dominant public complaint is crowding rather than danger. The Lake Harriet loop trail carries more than 1,600 public reviews, with usage repeatedly described as heavy. A corridor that residents avoid does not generate that pattern. Coverage of the break-in wave captured the counter-current in the ring neighborhoods:

"We think we live in a bubble here, but I guess not." Linden Hills resident, after a neighborhood break-in

One national piece (NBC, citing FBI framing) notes assaults on runners frequently occur in daylight and taper in early-morning hours. PI retains it rather than discarding it, and notes it cuts against the darkness intuition. It is national and general, and it is not evidence about this parkway. [A]

The neighborhood's own 2026 civic agenda is not about crime, and PI assesses that as a meaningful negative indicator. Lynnhurst Neighborhood Association and Southwest Voices coverage through the year centers on the Lynnhurst Park master plan and the proposed removal of the wading pool, the Oliver Avenue storm sewer works, Penn Avenue bridge redecking, and Porchfest on 25 June. Residents organising around park amenities and construction, rather than around safety, is what a low-threat neighborhood looks like from the inside. [A]

Southwest Voices is the best local source identified. It covers East Harriet, Lynnhurst, Fulton and Linden Hills by name, works from City of Minneapolis data, and holds an explicit anti-fear-mongering editorial stance. Per Section 01, its article dates must be checked individually rather than trusted from a search summary.

04 Resident-Forum Layer. Not Collected

This layer is entirely open and nothing in this annex covers it. Two separate barriers, and the second is not specific to this environment:

  • Nextdoor is behind a login wall. No authenticated session is available here, and nextdoor.com is blocked at the network policy.
  • Reddit refuses Anthropic's crawler outright. A domain-restricted search returns a hard refusal naming the user agent: "The following domains are not accessible to our user agent: ['reddit.com']." No Claude session can reach Reddit. Only the owner's own browser can.

Reddit, owner's browser: site:reddit.com/r/minneapolis Lynnhurst · "Lake Harriet" safe · "Lake Harriet" running · site:reddit.com/r/TwinCities "East Harriet"

Capture rule. Record the event date and whether the account is firsthand or a repost for every hit. In window and firsthand, retain. Older than the window, or hearsay, discard. Section 01 is what happens when that rule is not applied at collection time. This layer is the one source that would surface incidents too small to reach the press, which is precisely the gap the Section 02 null result leaves open.
