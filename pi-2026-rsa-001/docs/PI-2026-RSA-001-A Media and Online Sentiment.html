01 Dating Failures Found in This Assessment

Three claims about this neighborhood proved misdated. All three were excluded.

1. The carjacking cluster. "Five of the seven carjackings in the neighborhoods that ring Lake Harriet happened between November 14 and 25." Carried in Rev A as in-window. It is November 2021, roughly four years outside the window. Corroborated by Axios Twin Cities (9 Nov 2021), MinnPost (Nov 2021) and the Star Tribune 2021 carjackings-by-neighborhood map. The Southwest Voices piece dates its own comparison cluster to "a ten-day stretch of November 2021." Tagged [S] rather than [V]: the byline could not be opened, since southwestvoices.news is blocked by this environment's network policy, so the dating rests on corroboration.

2. The aggravated-robbery warning. An MPD 5th Precinct alert describing victims as "alone or with one other person, either walking or in or near a vehicle" reads as directly on point for a solo runner. It attributes the warning to Inspector Katie Blackwell, who commanded the 5th Precinct from 2019 until August 2023 and is now MPD Acting Chief. The alert is at best three years stale and names Lowry Hill East and Whittier, neither of which is in the ring. Excluded on both date and geography.

3. The band shell shooting. A search summary returned this as "October 2026." Charging records place it at 24 October 2023. The same summary described a completed murder where no one was injured. Excluded as current, retained as history.

PI assesses date verification, not collection volume, as the binding constraint on this assessment. Each of the three would have passed a casual read, and each described exactly the kind of incident the owner is asking about. The rule that caught all three is the same one Section 04 imposes on the forum layer: capture the date first, then decide whether the item counts.

03 Neighborhood Sentiment

The lake reads as safe and heavily used, and the dominant public complaint is crowding rather than danger. The Lake Harriet loop trail carries more than 1,600 public reviews, with usage described as heavy and one 2025 review noting "more people on the trail than I usually see walking in a week." A corridor residents avoid does not generate that pattern.

Coverage of the break-in wave captured the counter-current in the ring neighborhoods:

"We think we live in a bubble here, but I guess not." Linden Hills resident, after a neighborhood break-in

One national piece (NBC, citing FBI framing) notes assaults on runners frequently occur in daylight and taper in early-morning hours. PI retains it rather than discarding it, and notes it cuts against the darkness intuition. It is national and general, and it is not evidence about this parkway.

The neighborhood's own 2026 civic agenda is not about crime, and that is a finding. Lynnhurst Neighborhood Association and Southwest Voices coverage through 2026 centers on the Lynnhurst Park master plan and the proposed removal of the wading pool, the Oliver Avenue storm sewer works, Penn Avenue bridge redecking, and Porchfest on 25 June. Residents organising around park amenities and construction, rather than around safety, is what a low-threat neighborhood looks like from the inside. [A]

Southwest Voices remains the best local source identified. It covers East Harriet, Lynnhurst, Fulton and Linden Hills by name, works from City of Minneapolis data, and holds an explicit anti-fear-mongering editorial stance. Per Section 01, its article dates must be checked individually rather than trusted from a search summary.

04 Resident-Forum Layer. Not Collected

This layer is entirely open and nothing in this annex covers it. Two separate barriers, and the second is new information:

  • Nextdoor is behind a login wall. No authenticated session is available to this environment, and nextdoor.com is blocked at the network policy.
  • Reddit refuses Anthropic's crawler outright. A domain-restricted search returns a hard refusal: "The following domains are not accessible to our user agent: ['reddit.com']." This is not a limitation of this container. No Claude session can reach Reddit. Only the owner's own browser can.

Nextdoor, logged in, 55419: Lake Harriet Parkway · runner · jogger · prowler · car break-in · window smashed · package · porch · suspicious · catalytic

Reddit, owner's browser: site:reddit.com/r/minneapolis Lynnhurst · "Lake Harriet" safe · "Lake Harriet" running · site:reddit.com/r/TwinCities "East Harriet"

Capture rule. Record the date and whether the account is firsthand or repost for every hit. In-window and firsthand, retain. Older than the window, or hearsay, discard. Section 01 is what happens when that rule is not applied at collection time.
