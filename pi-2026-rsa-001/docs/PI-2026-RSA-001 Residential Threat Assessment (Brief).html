The run — what actually drives the risk [A]

  • Darkness is the variable, not the lake. On 14 Sep, Minneapolis civil twilight begins ~06:20; by late autumn a 5:30 a.m. start is in full dark for the entire run. The route's risk profile changes month to month while the route itself does not.
  • Predictability compounds it. A fixed route at a fixed time is the one factor fully under the owner's control and the one that most reduces the value of a low base rate. Vary start time ±20 min and alternate direction.
  • Patrol seam. The parkway is Park Board property patrolled by MPRB Park Police; the adjacent residential blocks are MPD 5th Precinct. Park Patrol Agents concentrate on lakes and parkways in summer months. A 5:30 a.m. winter run sits at the thinnest point of both coverages.
  • Offender-presence overlap — encounter risk, not violence risk. The documented vehicle-break-in crews operate 2–7 a.m. in these exact neighborhoods. A 5:30 a.m. run overlaps that window. This does not raise the violence estimate — these offenders target parked cars and flee people. It means an encounter with someone committing a crime is plausible. The correct response is disengagement and distance, never confrontation. Category C is not inflated on this basis.
  • Structural persistence factor. Minnesota's "Raise the Age" law took effect 1 Aug 2026: children under 13 cannot be charged, prosecuted, or detained, with no exceptions. Offenders in the local sprees have included 11-year-olds. Expect the property-crime pattern to persist rather than resolve through enforcement. [V]
  • Conditional — resolve before finalizing. The rape-category increase above is the most activity-relevant trend line, but its weight depends on the runner's own risk profile, which this assessment does not have on record. [?]
