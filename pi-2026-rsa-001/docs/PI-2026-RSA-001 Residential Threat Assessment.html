02 Scope, Method and Quality Control

Two filters, strict, applied in order. (1) Date: the event occurred between 14 September 2025 and 14 September 2026 inclusive, a 365-day span. Publication date never qualifies an item; only the event date does. (2) Distance: the event occurred within approximately 5 miles of the subject address. Distances are estimated from Minneapolis street geography and are approximate. No geocoding service was reachable from this environment. [A]

Every item was verified against a primary report before inclusion. That pass removed six items and corrected three factual errors, two of which had already been published in an earlier revision of this document. Section 07 logs all of it.

What this scope costs, stated plainly. A strict 365-day window removes the only exact time, place and activity match in the entire file: a solo runner grabbed from behind and chased at 05:30 on West Lake Harriet Parkway near Sheridan Avenue South, September 2015. It also removes the July 2017 escalation series on this lake corridor, in which a man knocked a woman off her bicycle and assaulted her and a second man grabbed two women four days later, and the November 2020 minivan that left the parkway and struck a jogger.

Those three are the strongest activity-specific evidence that exists for this location. PI assesses the resulting picture as less informative about the question being asked, not safer. Category C is now carried by structural conditions rather than by precedent.

04 The Parkway Itself

The search was run repeatedly and from several angles: the parkway by name, the band shell, the boat launch, the rec center, MPRB Park Police incident summaries, and neighborhood press.
