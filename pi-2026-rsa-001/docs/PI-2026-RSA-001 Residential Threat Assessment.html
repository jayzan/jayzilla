02 Scope and Method

Two filters, both strict, applied in this order. An item must pass both. (1) Date: the event occurred between 14 September 2025 and 14 September 2026. Publication date does not qualify an item; the event date does. (2) Distance: the event occurred within approximately 5 miles of the subject address. Distances are estimated from Minneapolis street geography and are approximate. No geocoding service was reachable from this environment. [A]

Every item below was individually date-verified against a primary report before inclusion. That step removed five items that would otherwise have qualified (Section 07).

What this scope costs, stated plainly. A strict 365-day window removes the only exact time, place and activity match in the entire file: a solo runner grabbed from behind and chased at 05:30 on West Lake Harriet Parkway near Sheridan Avenue South in September 2015. It also removes the July 2017 escalation series on this lake corridor and the November 2020 minivan strike that injured a jogger on this parkway. Those three are the strongest activity-specific evidence that exists for this location, and none of them is in window.

PI assesses the resulting picture as less informative about the specific question asked, not safer. Category C is now carried by structural conditions rather than by precedent. Rev C retains the historical record.

04 The Parkway Itself

No violent incident, robbery, assault, or exposure report was located on the Lake Harriet parkway or its adjoining blocks at any point inside the 365-day window. The search was run repeatedly and from several angles: the parkway by name, the band shell, the boat launch, the rec center, Park Police incident summaries, and neighborhood press. Every Lake Harriet result returned either an incident from 2023 or earlier, or concert programming. The nearest qualifying event of any kind is the 1 May vehicle collision at roughly one mile.

This is a null result and it is reported as one. It means no incident was reported in an accessible source. It is not a certification that none occurred, and it cannot be upgraded to one until the two data requests at Section 08 return. The City incident-level dataset, which is the record that would actually settle this, could not be queried from this environment.
